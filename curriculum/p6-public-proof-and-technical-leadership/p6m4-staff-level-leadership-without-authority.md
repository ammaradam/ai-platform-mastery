# P6M4 · Staff-level leadership without authority

**Pillar:** [Public proof and technical leadership](README.md) · **Time:** ~6 h · **Pass mark:** 80% on the quiz

## Learning objectives

- Describe staff engineer archetypes and pick yours
- Use writing, sponsorship and influence to lead across teams
- Decide between the staff IC and manager tracks with evidence

## Lesson

## Staff archetypes (Will Larson)
- **Tech Lead**: guides one team's approach and execution.
- **Architect**: direction and quality across a critical area.
- **Solver**: parachutes into hard problems.
- **Right Hand**: extends an executive's leadership across the org.
Your current role (platform direction, governance, coaching DS) mixes Tech
Lead and Architect.

## Staff work (Tanya Reilly's framing)
Three pillars: **big-picture thinking** (strategy, vision, technical
direction), **project execution** (leading hard, ambiguous projects to done),
and **levelling up others** (mentoring, setting standards, reviews). Much of it
is "glue work", make it visible and tie it to outcomes.

## Influence tools
- Written strategy and vision docs (connect to p6m1).
- Getting sponsors: managers and execs who will spend capital on your ideas.
- Being "in the room": and earning it by bringing data.
- Making others successful (coaching DS to "drive on the roads").

## IC vs manager
Managers own people outcomes (hiring, performance, growth); staff ICs own
technical outcomes across teams. Both lead. Look at your energy: do you prefer
1:1s and growing people, or deep technical direction? Your coaching + visionary
style fits either; the job you pick should match what you want to spend most
days doing.

## Quiz

Answer without notes first. Answers are in the collapsible section below.

**Q1.** Which staff archetype extends an executive's leadership across the organisation?

- A) Architect
- B) Tech Lead
- C) Right Hand
- D) Solver

**Q2.** Which is NOT one of Tanya Reilly's three pillars of staff work?

- A) Big-picture thinking
- B) People management
- C) Project execution
- D) Levelling up others

**Q3.** What's the best way to make glue work count toward promotion?

- A) Make it visible and tie it to outcomes
- B) Delegate it
- C) Do it quietly
- D) Stop doing it

**Q4.** The clearest difference between manager and staff IC tracks is...

- A) ICs can't influence strategy
- B) Salary
- C) Managers own people outcomes; staff ICs own technical outcomes across teams
- D) Only managers lead

<details><summary>Answers and explanations</summary>

- **Q1: C** — Larson's Right Hand acts as an extension of a senior leader.
- **Q2: B** — Staff ICs lead without line management.
- **Q3: A** — Unseen glue work is rarely rewarded.
- **Q4: C** — Both lead, through different levers.

</details>

## Exercises

### Technical vision doc (core)

Write a 2-page, 12-18 month technical vision for AI at Pickles (or a target employer), platform, evals, governance.

**Deliverable:** Vision doc.

**Done when:**
- [ ] States current state, target state, and 3 bets with how you'll measure them

### Archetype and track decision (core)

Log a week of your work by archetype and by IC vs management activity; write a 1-page decision on which track to pursue next.

**Deliverable:** Activity log + decision.

**Done when:**
- [ ] Decision references the log, not just preference
